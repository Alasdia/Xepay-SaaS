The Xepay commission is never refunded, in full or in part, regardless of the reason for the refund. Stripe itself never returns its own processing fees on a refund; the Xepay commission follows the same principle. In practice, the amount paid by the end Customer is refunded normally, but the Merchant's account may temporarily show a negative balance equal to the commission that was not returned — this balance is automatically settled against the next payout, as natively supported by Stripe Connect.

The Merchant chooses, for each refund, who bears this non-returned amount: refunding the full amount paid by the end Customer (the Merchant then absorbs the commission via the negative balance described above), or refunding only the net amount it actually received (in which case the end Customer does not recover the full original payment).
