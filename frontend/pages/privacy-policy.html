1Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ALASDIA TECHNOLOGIES LLC, operator of the Xepay platform (“Xepay”, “we”), collects, uses, shares and protects the personal data of users of the Service — whether Merchants holding a Xepay Account or end Customers making a payment through a Xepay payment link.

By using the Service, you accept the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree with them, we invite you not to use the Service.

2Data controller

ALASDIA TECHNOLOGIES LLC

Operator of the Xepay platform

Data protection email: privacy@alasdia.com

3Data we collect

Xepay only collects the data necessary to operate the Service: opening and securing an Account, verifying a Merchant's identity as required by applicable payment services regulation, processing transactions initiated through a payment link, and providing technical and commercial support. We distinguish three broad categories of data below, depending on whether it is provided directly by the Merchant, generated automatically through use of the Service, or related to the end Customers paying through a Xepay link.

a) Data provided by the Merchant

  • Identification data: first name, last name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), phone number where applicable.
  • Business data: company name, country of operation, industry, information declared when opening a Stripe Connect account.
  • Identity verification (KYC) data: identity documents and supporting evidence submitted to Stripe as part of regulatory compliance — these documents pass through Xepay solely for technical relay to Stripe and are not durably retained on our servers beyond what is strictly technically necessary.
  • Banking data necessary for payouts, managed and stored exclusively by Stripe.

b) Data generated through use of the Service

  • Transaction history, payment links created, usage and performance statistics.
  • Technical logs: IP address, device and browser type, connection timestamps, pages viewed.
  • Data relating to API calls and webhooks configured by the Merchant.

c) Data relating to end Customers

  • Name, email address and payment information strictly necessary to complete a transaction through a payment link, collected and processed directly by Stripe. Xepay never has access to full card numbers.

4Sign in with Google

In addition to email/password sign-in, Xepay offers a “Sign in with Google” option, built on Google Identity Services (GIS) and the OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect protocol provided by Google.

a) Google data requested and obtained

When you choose to sign in with your Google account, Xepay asks Google for access to the following scopes, and only these:

ScopeData obtainedUse by Xepay
openidUnique Google identifier of your accountVerify your identity and link the sign-in to your Xepay Account
emailYour Google email address and its verification statusIdentify or create your Xepay Account, send you communications related to the Service
profileBasic profile information (name) provided by Google during authenticationPre-fill your Xepay profile; this information is not retained beyond what is necessary to create the Account

Xepay does not obtain or request any other data from your Google account (contacts, calendar, files, emails, location, etc.). Only your email address is actually retained and used to durably identify your Xepay Account.

b) Exclusive purpose and no advertising use

Data obtained through your Google account is used exclusively to authenticate you and create or link your Xepay Account. It is never used for advertising, marketing profiling, resale, or transfer to third parties other than the subprocessors strictly necessary for the operation of the Service (see the “Data sharing” section).

Xepay's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

c) Retention and deletion of Google data

Data obtained through Google is retained under the same rules as the other data in your Account (see the “Retention period” section). You can at any time:

  • revoke Xepay's access to your Google account from myaccount.google.com/permissions;
  • request deletion of the data associated with your Xepay Account by contacting us at privacy@alasdia.com, or directly from your Account settings.

Revoking Google access does not automatically delete your Xepay Account; it only disables the ability to sign in again via Google (email/password sign-in remains available if it has been configured).

5Purposes of processing

Every piece of data we collect serves a specific purpose, directly tied to providing the Service or to an obligation Xepay or Stripe are subject to. We never use your data for purposes incompatible with those described below, and we never sell it to third parties for advertising purposes. Specifically, the data collected is used to:

  • create and administer your Account and Workspace;
  • enable payment processing and the disbursement of funds via Stripe;
  • ensure the security of the Service, prevent fraud and detect misuse;
  • provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • inform you of changes to the Service, security incidents or contractual changes;
  • produce aggregated usage statistics and improve the Service;
  • comply with the legal and regulatory obligations to which Xepay or Stripe are subject (anti-money laundering, tax obligations, etc.).

6Legal bases

Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, the processing of your data is based in particular on:

  • Performance of the contract binding you to Xepay (provision of the Service, payment processing);
  • Compliance with legal obligations (KYC/AML compliance, tax obligations);
  • Xepay's legitimate interest in ensuring security, fraud prevention and improving the Service;
  • Your consent, where required, notably for certain marketing communications or non-essential cookies.

7Data sharing

Xepay never sells your personal data. It may be shared, strictly to the extent necessary, with:

RecipientReason for sharing
Stripe, Inc. (payment subprocessor)Payment processing, identity verification, disbursement of funds
Hosting and infrastructure providersHosting of the application and databases
Transactional email providersAccount notifications, security alerts
Competent authoritiesWhere required by law or in response to a valid judicial request

Each of these providers is contractually bound to protect your data and to use it only for the purposes authorized by Xepay. We select our subprocessors based on their security and compliance guarantees, and we systematically limit the data shared to what is strictly necessary for them to perform their role. Xepay remains responsible for the processing of your data even when it is partially delegated to one of these providers, and never shares your data with third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

8International data transfers

Some of our providers (notably Stripe) process data outside your country of residence, including in the United States. When such transfers take place, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (standard contractual clauses, recognized certifications or equivalent mechanisms) in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.

9Retention period

Your data is retained for the duration of your Account, then archived for an additional period necessary to comply with our legal obligations (notably accounting and tax obligations), generally between five (5) and ten (10) years depending on the nature of the data and the applicable jurisdiction. Beyond that, your data is irreversibly deleted or anonymized.

10Data security

Xepay implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure, including: password encryption, encrypted communications (HTTPS/TLS), role-based access control within Workspaces, two-factor authentication available on Accounts, and logging of sensitive access.

As no system is entirely infallible, we encourage you to protect your own credentials and to report any suspicious activity on your Account to us without delay.

11Cookies and similar technologies

The Xepay website and application use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: maintaining your login session, securing the Service — these cookies cannot be disabled without preventing the Service from working.
  • Audience measurement cookies: anonymized understanding of site usage in order to improve its usability.

You can configure your browser to refuse non-essential cookies; this may however limit certain site features.

12Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: obtain a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • Right of rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure: request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations;
  • Right to restriction of processing in certain cases provided for by law;
  • Right to data portability for the data you provided to us;
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, notably for direct marketing purposes;
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@alasdia.com. We will respond to your request as soon as possible and in accordance with applicable legal deadlines.

13Minors

The Service is intended for professionals and adult individuals. Xepay does not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can delete it.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, technical or organizational changes. Any material change will be notified to you by email or via the dashboard before it takes effect. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
